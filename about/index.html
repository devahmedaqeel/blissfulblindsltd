About Blissful Blinds

Headquartered at 75 Ringwood Bretton, Peterborough (PE3 9SR), Blissful Blinds is dedicated to bringing luxury, bespoke window coverings to homes and businesses across Peterborough and surrounding PE postcodes.

Our Heritage & Commitment to Peterborough

At Blissful Blinds, we believe every room deserves perfect light control, elegant style, and privacy. From modest kitchen window roller blinds to comprehensive commercial office installations, our ethos centers around exceptional customer care, transparent pricing, and expert craftsmanship.

Every consultation is carried out directly in your home, allowing you to examine fabric swatches under your exact room lighting before making a decision.
